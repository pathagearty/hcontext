Method & limits

What the comparison does—and does not—prove

Controlled

Same conditions

Request, actor, tenant, site, snapshot, Decision Profile, Manufacturing Agent contract, evidence schema, and tool plane are frozen for both arms.

Private

Score after results

The runtime paths do not receive expected answers. The synthetic evaluator reads its separate answer key only after both results exist.

Estimated

Cost is not billing

The app can estimate variable model cost from provider tokens and an approved pricing catalog. Azure Cost Management remains the later billing source of truth.

Bounded

Small synthetic MVP

Fifteen designed cases can reveal implementation and safety failures. They cannot establish production accuracy, compliance, or customer ROI.

HexaContext manufacturing MVP · Synthetic data only · Human quality review remains the final authority
